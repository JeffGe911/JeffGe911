## The problem

BaZi and Western astrology are often presented as separate reports full of unfamiliar terminology. I built DuoSky to let people explore the two perspectives through one readable, trilingual experience, with explanations they can inspect.

The product is for cultural exploration and reflection. Its calculations and interpretation rules do not establish scientifically validated predictions.

## What I own

I own product scope, architecture, UX, interpretation policy, acceptance criteria, release decisions, and iteration. I use AI coding tools to accelerate implementation and review while retaining responsibility for the product and its behavior.

The product runs on Cloudflare Workers. Core calculations and interpretations follow deterministic code and rules; they are not freshly generated by a chatbot for each reading.

## Product and engineering decisions

- **Start simple, add precision:** users can start with a date and add time and place. Unknown birth time limits time-sensitive conclusions instead of presenting reference values as certain.
- **Expose the reasoning:** chart explanations and side-by-side evidence comparisons help readers understand where the traditions agree, complement one another, or differ.
- **Keep the experience coherent:** birth inputs, historical time zones, place resolution, chart calculations, language changes, and reading depth must remain consistent through the user journey.
- **Make geography useful:** birthplace resolution, lazy-loaded place data, GIS-based portraits, and geometry fallbacks support a personal, shareable experience.
- **Design the takeaway:** shareable portraits omit names, birth dates, and raw birth times. Reading and navigation changes are evaluated as product behavior, alongside computation checks.

## Measurement & iteration

I built product analytics and feedback workflows to investigate feature use and reading behavior, with validation around data refreshes and metric definitions. QA activity, background sessions, and intended reading activity need different interpretations.

I use feedback and those measurements to prioritize changes and define how to evaluate them. Regression scenarios check consistency across chart profiles, settings, languages, and time periods. Test-coverage counts are not prediction-accuracy or user-growth metrics.
